How to spot a hidden leak early

  1. Home
  2. Blog
  3. Hidden leaks
Featured image placeholder
Maintenance 12 Oct 2026By Alex Morgan4 min read

Small leaks waste water, damage your home and push up your bills. The good news is that most are easy to catch if you know what to look for.

1. Check your water meter

Turn off every tap and appliance, then note the meter reading. If it has changed after an hour, you may have a leak somewhere on the property.

2. Look for damp and stains

Discoloured patches on walls or ceilings, peeling paint and a musty smell are classic warning signs of a hidden leak.

Fixing a leak early is almost always cheaper than repairing the damage it causes later.

3. Listen for running water

In a quiet house, a hissing or trickling sound when nothing is on is worth investigating immediately.

When to call a professional

  • The meter keeps moving with everything switched off
  • You can see damp but cannot find the source
  • Your water pressure has dropped suddenly
